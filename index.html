VOCATIONAL PROGRAMS

Our Courses

Specialized skill development designed to prepare women for modern employment and entrepreneurship.

Computer Operator Assistant

This course provides practical training in essential computer and digital skills, including MS Office, Word, Access, PowerPoint, and Typing Master. Students also learn graphic designing tools such as Photopea and Adobe Photoshop, along with InPage, PDFGear, Cursor, and modern online communication platforms including Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom. The course further introduces students to ChatGPT, Lovable, Visual Studio, website development, and n8n automation, preparing them for modern digital and professional work environments.

6 MONTH DURATION →

Beautician

The Beautician course provides practical training in beauty care, skincare, hair care, makeup, mehndi and salon management. Students also learn essential salon management skills, preparing them to work confidently in beauty salons or start their own professional beauty business.

6 MONTH DURATION →

Basic Dress Designing

This course is designed for beginners and provides practical training in basic dress designing and stitching skills. Students learn from the fundamentals, including fabric selection, measurements, cutting, stitching, basic patterns, and dress designing techniques, helping them develop the skills needed to create and design beautiful garments.

1 YEAR DURATION →

Diploma in Vocational Girls

This diploma provides practical training in a variety of vocational and creative skills, including Arts & Crafts, Cutting & Stitching, Basic Computer Skills, and Basic Beautician techniques. Students also learn designing and kurashiya skills, helping them develop practical abilities for employment, self-employment, and creative work.

2 YEAR DURATION →
